虛擬生成指南（十）：光線應用



前言

在虛擬生成領域，光線的運用至關重要。不同的光線可以創造出不同的氛圍，並傳達特定的情感。
本篇將介紹十種常見的光線應用，並提供每種光線的範例圖，幫助您在虛擬創作中靈活運用這些光線。

1. 自然光（Natural Light）

自然光來自太陽，光線柔和，色彩真實。
咒語（prompt）：A serene natural outdoor scene with sunlight streaming through the trees, creating a soft and warm atmosphere. The light is natural and gentle, illuminating the forest path.

2. 環境光（Ambient Light）

環境光均勻分布，通常來自多個光源，營造柔和的氛圍。
咒語（prompt）：A cozy living room with multiple light sources such as lamps and ceiling lights, creating a warm and inviting ambient light. The light is evenly distributed, making the room feel comfortable and homey.

Midjourney

3. 主光（Key Light）

主光源通常最亮，決定主要陰影和亮度的對比。
咒語（prompt）：A dramatic portrait of a person with one strong light source illuminating one side of their face, creating deep shadows on the other side. The key light highlights the facial features with high contrast.

Midjourney

4. 補光（Fill Light）

補光用於減少陰影和對比，通常比主光源柔和和暗淡。
咒語（prompt）：A portrait of a person with a secondary light source reducing the shadows on their face, creating a more balanced and natural look. The fill light softens the shadows and complements the key light.

5. 背光（Back Light）

背光從背後照射，突出輪廓，營造立體效果。
咒語（prompt）：A silhouette of a person standing against a bright light source behind them, creating a glowing outline around their body. The back light emphasizes the shape and separation from the background.

6. 邊緣光（Rim Light）

邊緣光圍繞在主體邊緣，強調輪廓和分離背景。
咒語（prompt）：A portrait of a person with a light source illuminating the edges of their face and hair, creating a subtle glowing outline. The rim light enhances the separation from the background and adds depth.

7. 側光（Side Light）

側光從側面照射，強調質感和立體感，創造深邃陰影。
咒語（prompt）：A dramatic scene with a person illuminated from one side, casting deep shadows on the opposite side. The side light highlights textures and creates a sense of depth and mystery.

Midjourney

8. 柔光（Soft Light）

柔光擴散光源，減少陰影，營造溫暖、柔和的氛圍。
咒語（prompt）：A gentle portrait of a person with diffused light surrounding them, reducing harsh shadows and creating a warm, soft atmosphere. The soft light gives a tender and flattering effect.

9. 硬光（Hard Light）

硬光集中光源，創造強烈陰影和高對比度，通常給人冷峻感。
咒語（prompt）：A high-contrast scene with a person under a bright, focused light source, creating sharp shadows and highlights. The hard light gives a bold and dramatic effect, emphasizing details.

10. 聚光燈（Spotlight）

聚光燈集中在特定區域的強光，突出重點，常用於舞台和劇場效果。
咒語（prompt）：A stage scene with a single spotlight focused on a performer, creating a bright circle of light around them while the surroundings are dark. The spotlight draws attention to the performer and creates a dramatic effect.
